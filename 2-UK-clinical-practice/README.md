## 🏆 Highlight: National Presentation (Project 4)

**Title:** *Incisional Negative Pressure Wound Dressings (iNPWD) for Spinal Fusions*  
**Venue:** Scottish Spine Surgeons 14th Annual Meeting (Nov 2023)  
**Key Finding:** **0% Surgical Site Infection (SSI)** in iNPWD group vs **15.4%** in matched controls.  
**Impact:** Contributed to emerging evidence for spinal surgery guidance; advocated for prospective studies.  
**Evidence:** [Presentation Slides](./project%204%20-%20Incisional%20Negative%20Pressure%20Wound%20Dressings%20(iNPWD)%20for%20spinal%20fusions%20-%20national%20presentation.pdf) • [Conference Abstracts](./project%204%20-%20Scottish%20Spine%20Surgeons%2014th%20Annual%20Meeting%20-%20programme%20and%20abstracts.pdf) • [Endorsement](./consultants-endorsements/Consultant%20Neurosurgeon%20Mr%20Arora%20-%20project%204.pdf)

## 🤝 Team-Led Quality Improvements (Neurosurgery)

Led junior doctor teams through full QI cycles (Plan-Do-Study-Act) for:
1.  **VTE Prophylaxis:** Assessed risk assessment compliance and dosing accuracy.
2.  **Medication Reconciliation:** Audited HEPMA documentation completeness.
3.  **Anticoagulant Guidance:** Evaluated discharge planning for long-term ACO users.

**Outcome:** Interventions included departmental presentations, email reminders, and direct engagement.

## 📈 Independent Data Analysis (Plastic Surgery)

**Project 6: RACH CEPOD Utilization**  
Analyzed 94 emergency procedures to optimize staffing and training models.  
**Finding:** Proposed allocating a dedicated Consultant (2–3 mornings/week) for hand fractures and burns to improve standard of care.  
**Evidence:** [Analysis Presentation](./project%206%20-%20Analysis%20of%20RACH%20CEPOD%20utilization%20by%20Plastics%20Department.pptx) • [Endorsement](./consultants-endorsements/Consultant%20Plastic%20Surgeon%20Mrs%20Greenhowe%20-%20project%206.pdf)
